Research · Bargatin Group · 2026

Lightweight Microchannel Radiator for Orbital AI Data Centers

In vacuum, radiation is the only way to reject heat, so cooling networks for orbital data centers have to be redesigned around mass. I designed and fabricated a polymer microchannel radiator and manifold network, then optimized its geometry in simulation.

  • Modeled laminar flow with conjugate heat transfer and surface radiation in COMSOL Multiphysics (370 K inlet, HDPE properties), sweeping geometry to build a mass vs. power-dissipated Pareto frontier.
  • Found two near-optimal geometries; the circular-pipe case (0.22 cm radius) is the more manufacturable.
  • Swept thermal conductivity from 0.1 to 5000 W/m·K and found an optimum near 71 W/m·K: beyond it, heat spreads along the flow and radiator efficiency drops.
  • Developed a thermal-welding method that welds UHMWPE sheets over PFA tubing at 400°C, and a tube-closure approach to isolate punctured sections after a debris strike.
  • Presented at the Penn Summer Research Expo.
